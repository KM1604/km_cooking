# BANANA BREAD!!! AT WORK, DUDE!!!

## Ingredients
* 100% - bananas
* 1Tbsp melted butter per 50g banana
* 1 egg per 300g of banana (ish)
* 75% - flour
* 75% - sugar (part of this can be brown)
* 3% - baking soda
* dash or two of salt
* vanilla, cinnamon, molasses, cardamom, nutmeg, etc. as your heart leads.

## Cooking Instructions
* Preheat oven to 350
* mash bananas in big bowl
* add wets
* mash more
* add half of your mixed dry stuff
* mash more
* add the rest of the dry stuff
* mash more
* Dump in oiled, buttered, or greased pan
* bake for 60 mins

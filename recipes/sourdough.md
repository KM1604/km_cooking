# Sourdough Bread

## Starter

Three ingredients:
* flour (100%)
* water (80-100%)

Feed it every day it's not in the fridge. If you don't want to make bread in the next few days, just put it in the fridge.
Feed a minimum of 25g flour per non-fridge day, up to 50g.
Feed it 100g flour on the morning before you start bread.

### Notes about your starter
* It’ll sometimes get a weird clear thing on the top, or even greyish. This is fine.
* You start getting pretty colors in the liquid it’s time to throw it out.
* It should bubble each day you feed it.
* I like my starter to be a bit runnier than dough, but thick enough to have bubbles
* It should be sorta stringy if you try to stir it.

## Making Bread
Start this process morning on the day before you want bread.
### First morning over breakfast
* feed the starter 100g flour and water

### First day evening
* mix 540g of flour and 420g of warm water in a big bowl
* Cover the bowl with a towel, and let it sit at least a half hourish

### 30-60 minutes later
* pour in 200g of starter
* pour in 20g of salt

Your big bowl now has 540g flour, 420g water, 200g starter, and 20g salt. Mix it up well in the bowl, cover, then you’re done till the next morning. 

NOTE: Some people use their hands and pinch and fold it, some like to dump it on the counter (messier…be careful not to add too much flour to the dough if you do this), and some people just grab a spatula and poke, fold, and swirl it around for a while.

### Second Day Morning
*(or lunch, or once the dough looks doubled, play this by ear)*
* shape the dough
* It’s already mixed, so you’re not kneading it too much.
* You’ll likely need to add flour, this recipe makes a sorta wet dough, so don’t be afraid to add flour to the surface.
* As you work it, it’ll start sticking less.
* Your goal is to tuck the edges of the dough under, so the top has a nice smooth look to it.
* There are tons of videos online about “shaping” a sourdough loaf, go learn from people who know more than me.

### Once your dough is shaped
* cover it with a towel
* preheat your oven to 485F with cast iron dutch oven thing inside
* let sit and rise/proof again for 1h or so

### One Hour Later
*(some do this for longer, pay attention and adjust accordingly)*
* carefully pull your hot iron thing out of the oven
* carefully transfer your dough into the super hot dutch oven. 
* place back in the oven at 485 for 70 minutes. 

### 70 minutes later
Pull out your bread and let it cool on a cooling rack, then cut into it.
